# Chat-first home (/app/today)

The home screen is a conversation, not a dashboard.

- **Empty:** one large orb you can speak to, and a composer with only three things: a text box, **Talk**, and **Call**.
  No suggestion chips, no greeting card, no Today card, no call card.
- **Once you speak or type:** the orb shrinks to a small header and the conversation flows top to bottom, like a chat.
  **New chat** clears it. Typing text swaps Talk and Call for Send, as in a chat app.
- **Panels appear inside the conversation only when asked for**, by voice or by typing: History, Brain dump, Routines,
  what Stay remembers, Parked thoughts, Reminders, the Today list, and "Call me later". They are cards in the page flow
  (not modals) and close with Close or Escape. A finished brain dump or routine puts the Today list in the chat.
  Settings, Reports and Check-in are still pages ("open settings").
- **How a phrase becomes a panel:** `detectPanel()` in `src/lib/voice/voiceCommands.ts` (typed text and speech use the
  same parser), and the model's `open` action uses it too, so a spoken "show my routines" and a typed one do the same thing.
  Everything is also reachable without talking from the More menu.
- **What it does not do:** panels are not generated by a model. Each one is a fixed component that the conversation opens;
  there is no free-form generated UI.

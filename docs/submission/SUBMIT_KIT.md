I built a voice app for people who keep putting things off. It's called Stay.

I made it for AssemblyAI's voice hackathon.

The idea is simple. You say the thing you're avoiding, like "I can't start the email to my landlord." Stay doesn't lecture you. It gives you one small first step, then it waits.

What I tried to get right:
- It answers your question first, before it coaches.
- It waits while you think, even when you trail off mid-sentence.
- It speaks first when your timer ends. If you switch it on, it can check in on you twice a day, even when the app is closed, never at night. The message is written on your phone from your own list, so my server never sees what you're avoiding.
- It asks before it uses your microphone, and it tells you it's an AI.
- Your history stays on your device. It is not a doctor, it gives no medication advice, and it points to emergency help if you're in crisis.
